Flow A of 3

Price it first, write it up second

Two screens. The first holds only what moves the number and gets Dane or Jackson to a total on site in a handful of taps. The second is optional: contact details, scope lines with editable prices, and the push to GHL.

  • The total is the hero. The dark band at the top recalculates on every keystroke using the real cost-plus chain. Tap it to see labor, dump, overhead, profit, and card fee.
  • Five things to touch for a number: name, hours, loads, other costs, markup. Everything else waits on the Write-up screen.
  • Write-up breaks the total into lines. Lines start at their share of the total and each price is editable. A green bar confirms the lines still add up; it turns amber when they drift.
  • Custom lines are allowed and tagged, so the scope library can absorb the frequent ones later.
Try it: change hours or loads on Price, then switch to Write-up and edit a line price. Logo and palette are placeholders wired to tokens, so both can change without touching the screens.

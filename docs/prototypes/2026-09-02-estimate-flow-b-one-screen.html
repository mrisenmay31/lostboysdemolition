Flow B of 3

One screen, open what you need

Everything lives on a single scroll. The total stays pinned at the top while you type. The four pricing inputs are always open; customer, scope lines, and details are folded cards you expand only when the bid needs them.

  • Fastest to a number. Hours and loads sit side by side under the total. Nothing to switch, nothing to open.
  • Folded cards carry a count. "Scope lines · 3" tells you what is inside without opening it, so a saved quick bid and a full write-up look different at a glance.
  • The trade-off is length. With every card open this is a long page on a phone. The pinned total keeps the number in view, but the customer block ends up a few screens down.
  • Push to GHL is one button next to Save; it is enabled only once an email is entered inside the Customer card.
Try it: open Scope lines, then change Hours at the top and watch the line prices re-split. Logo and palette are token placeholders.

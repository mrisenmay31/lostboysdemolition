Four steps, one job each

A guided sequence: Customer, Scope, Price, Review. Each step is a single focused screen, and the last one shows the estimate the way the customer will see it in GHL before anything is sent.

  • Scope drives hours. Ticking a library item brings its default hours and loads; the Price step opens with those already summed. Custom lines start empty.
  • Review is the customer document. Lines carry their price share; each is editable right on the review, and the total is checked before Send.
  • Cleanest for written estimates, slowest for a number on the driveway: three screens stand between opening the app and seeing a total.
  • Progress is legible. The bar under each step name shows done, current, and remaining, so it is obvious where you left off after a phone call.
Try it: tap through Next. On Scope, toggle a line and watch the hours summary; on Review, edit a line price. Logo and palette are token placeholders.

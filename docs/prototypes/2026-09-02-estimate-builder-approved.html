Job, Client, Financial, Review

Flow C with your step order and fields. Scope on the Job step names the customer's lines; hours and dumps are entered once on Financial; the Review step shows the estimate as the customer will see it, with each line's price editable.

  1. Job — job name, address, job type, job scope (library or custom), estimate date, start date if known, notes.
  2. Client — client name, client type, business name (contractors only), phone, email.
  3. Financial — total bid band on top; direct costs: total labor hours, dumps, other job costs with a tap-to-expand breakdown; then profit margin chips with true margin shown.
  4. Review — the same total band, the customer document with editable line prices, and the notes as the description.
  • Line prices start as an even split of the total across the scope lines (scope carries no hours in this flow) and each is editable; the bar under the table confirms the lines still add up.
  • Business name appears only when Client type is Contractor.
  • Record-only is left out per your call; logo and palette are token placeholders.
Try it: add a scope chip on Job, switch Client type to Contractor, change hours on Financial, then edit a line price on Review.
